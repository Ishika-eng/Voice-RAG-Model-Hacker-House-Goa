Speak, and it will listen for the truth — not just an answer.

Ask a question out loud in Hindi. Voice RAG transcribes it, searches MSMARCO-XI with three chunking strategies running at once, and answers only from what it actually finds in the retrieved context — or tells you plainly when it can't.

Four stages, every time

Nothing about an answer is a black box — each question passes through the same four checkpoints, in order, and any of them can end the request with a plain refusal instead of a guess.

01

Transcribe

Sarvam's speech model turns your spoken Hindi into text, with retries built in for a flaky connection.

02

Guard the input

Empty, unsafe, or oversized queries are rejected before anything gets retrieved.

03

Retrieve

Fixed-size, semantic, and passage-native chunking strategies are searched in parallel and fused by rank.

04

Generate & verify

Groq drafts an answer from the retrieved text only; a groundedness check can still veto it before you see it.
